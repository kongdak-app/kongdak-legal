Privacy Policy

At a glance

Kongdak has no server of ours, no account and no sign-up. Your settings and sleep records are kept on your iPhone, and we cannot see or receive them. The only information the app sends to anyone else is what the pass needs: your purchases, and the check that the pass is active. There are no ads and no tracking. The app has no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

Kongdak asks for no name, no birth date, no email and no photo. If you turn on "follow Mom's heart rate", the heart rate goes only from your Apple Watch to your own iPhone, and Kongdak keeps no history of it.

What the information is used for

Information is used for two things only. On your phone, it runs the night light and keeps your sleep log. For the pass, it is used to check whether your pass is active. It is not used for anything else.

What stays on your iPhone

Settings: the lamp color and size range, the row of sounds and the sounds you chose, the volumes, a tempo set by hand, the auto-off time, the bedtime reminder (on or off, and its time), the language, the widget background, and whether the first-time setup is finished.

Sleep records: when a sleep started and ended, the time-zone offset, whether it was a night or a nap, where it was started (app, widget or watch), and when the record was made or changed. A bedtime that is running is kept as its start time.

A small shared state, so that the widgets, the Lock Screen card and your Apple Watch can show and control things: whether the sound is on and which sounds, the tempo, the lamp color, the start of a running bedtime, whether the pass is on (and when a monthly period ends), and this week's "moons" (which nights have a record; no times and no durations).

All of this is stored in the app's own folder and the app's shared container (App Group) on this iPhone. iOS data protection keeps it encrypted until the phone has been unlocked once after a restart. We cannot see or receive any of it.

What leaves your iPhone

Only what the pass needs leaves the phone.

Apple handles the payment. Apple receives the purchase under your Apple Account, and Apple's own privacy policy applies to it.

RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) checks whether your pass is active. It is our service provider. The app sends RevenueCat the information below over an encrypted connection. It does so when the app starts, when it comes back to the front, and when you buy or restore.

A random app user ID created for this install. It is anonymous. It is not a name and not an Apple ID.

Apple's purchase records for this app: the product, the purchase and expiry times, the renewal state, and any refund.

The device's "identifier for vendor". This is an ID that Apple gives each developer on a device. It is not the advertising ID, and it cannot follow you across other companies' apps.

The device model, the iOS version, the app version and build, the app's bundle ID, the phone's preferred languages, and the App Store country.

Your IP address. As with any internet connection, it is visible to RevenueCat's servers.

What we can see: in RevenueCat's dashboard we see purchases by anonymous ID, country, product and dates. We cannot see who you are.

Never sent to anyone: sleep records, heart rate, settings, contacts and location. Nothing is typed into Kongdak, so nothing typed is sent either. The app has no access to the advertising ID.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

What is sent: the information listed under "What leaves your iPhone".

When and how: when the app starts, when it comes back to the front, and when you buy or restore, over an encrypted internet connection.

How to refuse: do not use the app, or delete it. The check starts when the app starts, so Kongdak cannot be used without it.

Mom's heart rate (Apple Watch)

Only the Apple Watch app reads heart rate. It uses the Health permission to read heart rate. The iPhone app does not use Health, and Kongdak writes nothing to Health.

The watch reads heart rate only while you have turned on "follow Mom's heart rate". When it is off, nothing is read. Without a pass it cannot be turned on.

While it is on, the watch keeps measuring through a workout-type session, so it also works with your wrist down. It sends the current beats per minute to your own paired iPhone. The iPhone turns that into the tempo of the light and sound. The tempo is always kept between 50 and 90.

Kongdak keeps no history of heart rate: no list and no times. While following, the reading and the tempo that follows it are held in memory only. They are not written to the app's files, not put in the widgets or the Lock Screen card, and not logged.

Heart rate is never sent to us, to RevenueCat, to advertisers or to anyone else. It only travels from the watch to your own iPhone.

When following stops, the light keeps the tempo it had at that moment as the current tempo, like a tempo set by hand. That is one number between 50 and 90.

The session is discarded, so Kongdak saves no workout. Apple Watch itself may keep the heart-rate readings it measured during that time in the Health app, as it does with its own measurements. That data is yours, under Apple's Health settings.

Health data is not used for advertising and is not sold. Kongdak does not store Health data in iCloud.

Notifications and permissions

Notifications: there is one optional notification, a daily bedtime reminder at the time you set. Permission is asked only when you turn it on. It is scheduled on the phone. There is no push server.

Health (heart rate, read) on the watch: asked only the first time you turn on following.

Not used: camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, Bluetooth permission, and tracking or the advertising ID.

The app plays audio in the background, with the screen locked, and shows a Live Activity. These need no permission dialog.

iPhone backups

If you have iPhone backups on (iCloud or a computer), they include this data. Backups belong to your own Apple Account or computer, and we cannot see them. A new iPhone gets the records back from a backup.

Keeping and deleting

Data on the phone is kept until you delete records or delete the app.

You can delete a record in the sleep log. You can undo for a few seconds. After that it is erased for good.

The sleep log shows the last three weeks. In this version, older records stay in the app's database on the phone, and you cannot open them or delete them one by one. There is no erase-all button in this version.

Deleting the app erases everything on the phone at once.

Deleting the app does not erase the purchase information that Apple and RevenueCat already hold. For RevenueCat's, see "Your rights" below.

Children

Kongdak is used by parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children, and a child does not use it. It asks for no name, no birth date, no email and no photo, of the baby or of anyone else.

A sleep record holds times, whether it was a night or a nap, and where it was started. It stays on the phone. We receive no information about your baby.

Your rights

Records on your phone: you can see and delete them in the sleep log, as described under "Keeping and deleting". The sleep log opens while you have a pass. Deleting the app erases everything, with or without a pass.

Pass information held by RevenueCat: you can ask to see it, correct it, delete it, or have its use stopped. Write to the contact below. We answer within 10 days. We see only an anonymous ID, so we may ask you for details of the purchase to find the record.

If you live in the EU or the UK

This section is about the pass information only. Sleep records, settings and heart rate stay on your own devices and never reach us.

Who is responsible: signokorea decides how the pass information is used. RevenueCat handles it for us as a service provider (a processor).

Why we may handle it: the pass information is needed to provide the pass. That means checking whether a pass is active and keeping a pass you bought working.

Your rights: you can ask us for a copy of the pass information, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to limit its use, or object to its use. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

If you live in California

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. You can ask us what pass information is held about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, at the contact below.

How we keep it safe

We run no server, and your records are not sent off the phone. Data on the phone is protected by iOS data protection, which is device encryption. The pass check is sent only over an encrypted connection. The app contains no secret key. We run security checks before each release.

Cookies, advertising IDs and analytics

The app has no cookies, no advertising ID, and no analytics or tracking SDK. It has no crash-reporting SDK.

Changes

If this policy changes, we post the change on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect. If a change would send more information off your phone, we tell you first, and we ask for your consent where the law requires it.
