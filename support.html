Kongdak Support

Kongdak is a baby night light for iPhone. The light on the screen brightens and dims to a heartbeat tempo, and a heartbeat sound, white noise, or both play at the same tempo. It is not a medical device, and it does not monitor your baby.

Common questions

Where are my records kept?

On your iPhone only. Kongdak has no server of ours, and we cannot see or receive your records. If you have iPhone backups on, your own backup includes them.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap Restore Purchases on the pass screen or in Settings.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

Open iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or in the app open Settings > Manage Subscription. After you cancel, the pass stays until the end of the period you paid for.

Does the sound keep playing when the screen is locked?

Yes. The sound keeps playing with the screen locked. It stops at the auto-off time you set.

What does "follow Mom's heart rate" need?

The Kongdak app on Apple Watch and the Health permission to read heart rate. The heart rate goes only from the watch to your own iPhone, and Kongdak keeps no history of it.

How do I erase my data?

Delete a record in the sleep log, or delete the app to erase everything on the phone at once.
