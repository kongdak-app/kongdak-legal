Terms of Use

The service

Kongdak is a baby night light for iPhone. The light on the phone screen brightens and dims to a heartbeat tempo, between 50 and 90 beats per minute. A heartbeat sound, white noise, or both play at the same tempo. Kongdak also has a sleep log kept on the phone, widgets, a Lock Screen card, Dynamic Island controls, and an Apple Watch app. The watch app is a remote and can, if you choose, follow Mom's heart rate.

You need an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 10 or later. The app is in English and Korean. There is no account and no sign-up.

These terms apply when you use Kongdak. The operator is signokorea, called "we" below.

Not a medical device

Kongdak is a night light. It is not a medical device, and it does not watch or monitor your baby. We do not promise that it will help your baby fall asleep or sleep longer. The adult using it decides where the phone sits and how loud and how bright it is.

Who uses it

Kongdak is used by parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children, and a child does not use it. It asks for no name, no birth date, no email and no photo.

The pass

Everything in Kongdak is paid. There is no free trial. Before you have a pass, you only see the first-time setup. One of its screens plays a short taste of the heartbeat sound. The taste stops by itself after at most one minute. It also stops when you leave that screen and when the app is no longer in front.

There is one pass, and it gives full access. There are two ways to get it. Lifetime is one payment and has no end. Monthly is a subscription that renews automatically every month. Both give exactly the same thing: all of the app.

The price is the App Store's price for your region. It is shown on the pass screen before you buy.

Payment and canceling

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The payment itself is handled by Apple through the App Store. Monthly renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

You can manage or cancel Monthly in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or in the app under Settings > Manage Subscription. After you cancel, the pass stays until the end of the period you paid for.

If you buy Lifetime while Monthly is still renewing, Apple does not stop the subscription by itself. The app tells you so and offers Manage Subscription. You need to cancel the monthly plan yourself.

Restore Purchases is on the pass screen and in Settings.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refunded purchase closes the pass at once.

When the pass ends

The pass ends when Monthly expires or when Apple refunds a purchase. The app then shows the pass screen again and the sound stops. If a bedtime is running, it is saved to the sleep log first. Your sleep records stay on the phone and are shown again once you have a pass.

If a monthly period runs out while the sound is playing, the app does not cut that night. The pass stays open until the sound stops, for at most 12 hours. Then the pass screen comes back.

Records and deleting

Your settings and sleep records are kept on your iPhone. We cannot see or receive them.

You can delete a record in the sleep log. You can undo for a few seconds. After that it is erased for good.

Sleep records stay on your iPhone until you delete them. You can delete one record in the sleep log, or all of them in Settings > "Delete all sleep records". Deleting the app erases everything on the phone at once.

The Privacy Policy has the details.

Apple Watch heart rate

If you turn on "follow Mom's heart rate", the Apple Watch app reads the heart rate of the person wearing the watch and sends it only to that person's own paired iPhone, where it sets the tempo of the light and sound. Kongdak keeps no history of it and sends it to no one else; the Privacy Policy explains this in full.
